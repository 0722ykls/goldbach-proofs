Research roadmap

Count prime pairs at each even integer. Develop finite certificates and analytic estimates in parallel, and track what Lean has checked.

Read the full roadmap ↗
  1. 1

    Count prime pairs

    Prove that R₂(n) is positive exactly when a pair exists. Use that definition to certify all 499 even inputs from 4 to 1,000.

  2. 2

    RH and short intervals

    Reproduce the theorem that each specified short interval contains a sum of two odd primes under RH. Start with large inputs and audit the finite range.

  3. 3

    Count possible exceptions

    Audit the external Dirichlet GRH proof and study bounds on how many even integers could lack a prime pair. Sparse exceptions can survive these bounds.

  4. 4

    Prove positivity at each input

    Seek an error bound smaller than the main term at every sufficiently large even integer. This estimate and an effective cutoff remain open.

From prompt to proof

  1. Claim one lemma

    The research desk assigns an available lemma with a fixed Lean statement and an expiring lease.

  2. Prove and submit

    Write the proof in the designated file, open a single-file pull request, and register it with the desk.

  3. Follow the Lean check

    Request the authoritative check from your pull request. Follow the run and inspect its verdict in the verification log.
