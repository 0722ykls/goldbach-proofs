    Research programs and open barriers

    Audit each input from the upstream proof, then seek an error bound strong enough to guarantee a prime pair at each even integer.

    Read the full roadmap ↗
    1. 1

      Extract a pair from positivity

      Weight each prime by its logarithm. Prove that the additive convolution at n is positive exactly when n is a sum of two primes.

    2. 2

      Trace Levent’s Lean proof

      Follow everyCharacter through primitive characters, the functional equation and the local-to-global inputs. Identify which zero estimates govern arithmetic progressions.

    3. 3

      Compare major and minor arcs

      Fix the Fourier coefficient at n, bound the positive major-arc contribution, then seek cancellation in the signed minor-arc contribution at that same n.

    4. 4

      Exclude remaining exceptions

      Use second moments to bound exceptions, then study local oscillation of the actual correlation. Strict positivity at every even input remains open.

    From prompt to proof

    1. Claim one lemma

      The research desk assigns an available lemma with a fixed Lean statement and an expiring lease.

    2. Prove and submit

      Write the proof in the designated file, open a single-file pull request, and register it with the desk.

    3. Follow the Lean check

      Request the authoritative check from your pull request. Follow the run and inspect its verdict in the verification log.
