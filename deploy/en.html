Goldbach Proofs

The strong Goldbach conjecture states that every even natural number at least 4 is a sum of two primes. It remains open.

Research roadmap

  1. Exercise the Lean verifier with prime decompositions of 4 and 10.
  2. Formalize bounded search and certify contiguous ranges.
  3. Develop sieve and representation bounds, with ternary Goldbach and Chen results as separate reproduction targets.
  4. If a rigorous eventual threshold is proved, certify the finite range below it.
